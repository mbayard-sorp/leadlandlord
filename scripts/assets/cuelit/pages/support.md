
# Cuelit Support

Need a hand? Email [support@cuelit.app](mailto:support@cuelit.app) and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days. Tell us which iPhone or iPad you use and your iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Getting started

**What does Cuelit do?**
Cuelit puts your script right at the lens, so you can read it and still look at your viewer. Record as many takes as you like, keep the good ones, then trim, caption and save the take without leaving the app.

**What do I need?**
An iPhone or iPad running iOS 27 or later.

**Where does the script appear?**
On iPhone, the script sits at the top of the screen, right under the front camera. On iPad, it sits along the edge where the front camera is. On iPhone Duo, prop the phone open and the script can scroll on the cover screen, right beside the rear camera, while the inner display shows the preview and controls.

**How do I get a script into Cuelit?**
Write it in Cuelit, import a file from Files, or send text from another app with the Share sheet and choose Cuelit.

## Recording

**Cuelit asks for the camera and microphone. Why?**
To record your takes. With Follow my voice on, the microphone also lets the script keep pace with you. Everything stays on your device. If you said no earlier, turn them on in the Settings app under Cuelit.

**The script moves too fast or too slow.**
Change the speed, text size and line height before you record. Cuelit saves them for each script. You can also turn on Follow my voice so the script follows you as you read and waits when you stop.

**My take was interrupted by a call or the app closing.**
Cuelit writes the video to disk as you record, so an interrupted take can usually be recovered the next time you open the app.

## Your takes

**Where are my videos stored?**
Only on your device, inside Cuelit. They are not uploaded anywhere, and they are not included in iCloud or device backups because of their size.

**How do I keep a take safe?**
Tap Save to Photos in Review or in the editor. Once it's in Photos, it's backed up the way your other photos are.

**I deleted Cuelit. Can I get my takes back?**
No. Deleting the app deletes every script, setting and take stored in it, and we never have a copy. Save anything you want to keep to Photos first.

## Editing

**Does editing change my original take?**
No. Your original is never changed. Edits are saved separately.

**Where do captions come from?**
From your script, timed to when you read each part. Every line is editable before you save or share.

**Can I add music?**
Yes. Pick one of Cuelit's own tracks, or add an audio file from Files. Music dips under your voice on its own.

## Subscription

**What does the subscription include?**
Cuelit is a subscription that unlocks the whole app, editing included. Yearly and monthly plans both start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

**How do I cancel?**
Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Cuelit. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

**I subscribed but Cuelit is asking me to subscribe again.**
Tap Restore purchase at the bottom of the subscription screen, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

**How do I get a refund?**
Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Privacy

Cuelit has no accounts and no login, and your videos, scripts and voice never leave your device. Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://cuelit.app/privacy-policy).
