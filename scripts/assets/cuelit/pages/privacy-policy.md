
# Cuelit Privacy Policy

**Effective:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

Cuelit is a teleprompter and video recording app made by Smart Orange Consulting, LLC ("we"). This policy explains what Cuelit does with your information. The short version: your videos, scripts and voice stay on your iPhone or iPad. The app never contacts our server. The only information we receive is the purchase record Apple sends us when you subscribe.

## Your videos, scripts and voice stay on your device

- **Videos you record** are stored only on your device. We never upload them, and we cannot see them. They are also excluded from iCloud and device backups because of their size.
- **Scripts and settings** are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Like other app data, they may be included in your iCloud or device backup, depending on your backup settings.
- **Edits** stay on your device too. When you edit a take, Cuelit keeps the edit, any voiceover you record, music you add and your logo on your device, next to your takes. Your original take is never changed. Like takes, edits are excluded from iCloud and device backups.
- **Face detection** happens on your device. While you record, Cuelit uses Apple's camera framework to notice where a face is in the picture. It uses this only for the Frame cue, which tells you when you drift out of frame, and for the self-view. It finds only a face's position, not who you are. Nothing about your face is stored or sent.
- **Follow my voice** happens on your device. When it is on, Cuelit listens through the microphone during a take so the script keeps pace with you. Apple's speech framework does the listening on your device. The first time, iOS may download Apple's speech model for your language once; that download is Apple's, and Cuelit sends nothing with it. No audio or transcript leaves your device, and Cuelit does not save what it hears.
- **Saving to Photos** only happens when you tap Save to Photos, in Review or in the editor. After that, the video is managed by the Photos app and your Photos settings.
- **Sharing** an edited video only happens when you choose where to send it in Apple's share sheet, with a captions file when captions are on.
- **Text and PDFs you send to Cuelit** from another app with the Share sheet are saved by Cuelit's Share extension into the app's own folder on your device (an App Group folder that only Cuelit can read), so Cuelit can open them. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Music that comes with Cuelit** is part of the app itself. Choosing a track downloads nothing and uses no network.
- **Readable aloud** uses Apple Intelligence on your device, where it is available. **Write with Siri** is Apple's own writing tool in the script editor: when you use it, Apple handles your text under Apple's privacy policy. Cuelit itself never sends your script to us or to anyone else.

Cuelit has no accounts and no login, and does not contain advertising, analytics, or tracking tools from any other company. The app and its Share extension never contact our server.

## Permissions

Cuelit asks for:

- **Camera and microphone**, to record your takes. Recordings stay on your device. With Follow my voice on, the microphone lets the script keep pace with you, on your device. It is also used when you record a voiceover in the editor; if you did not allow it when you first recorded, Cuelit asks the first time you record a voiceover.
- **Add to Photos**, only to save a take or an edited video when you ask. Cuelit cannot read your photo library.

Cuelit needs no permission for these, and gets only what you choose:

- **A photo or a logo you add to an edit.** You choose it in Apple's photo picker, which gives Cuelit only the picture you pick. Your logo is kept on your device for later edits until you remove it.
- **Music you add to an edit.** You choose an audio file in the Files app, and Cuelit copies only that file into the edit, on your device. Cuelit has no access to your Music library or Apple Music.

You can change these at any time in the Settings app.

## Subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, email address, Apple Account, or payment details.

When you start, renew, change, cancel, or get a refund for a Cuelit subscription, Apple sends us a notification about it. From it we keep a purchase record:

- an anonymous transaction ID that Apple assigns to your subscription
- which plan you chose, the price and currency, and your App Store country or region
- dates such as when the subscription started, renews, or ends
- whether a free trial or offer was used, whether it will renew, and whether it was refunded

We use these records only to understand how Cuelit is doing as a business, for example how many people subscribe and how many renew. We do not use them for advertising, we do not sell them, and we do not combine them with data from other sources.

The transaction ID does not contain your name or contact details. It could be connected to you only if you contact us with your App Store receipt or order ID, for example when asking for help with a purchase.

## Service providers

The purchase records are stored by service providers that host our systems: Vercel (the server that receives Apple's notifications) and Neon (the database). They process the data only on our behalf.

## How long we keep it

We keep purchase records for 7 years after your subscription ends, for business and tax records, and then delete them.

## Your choices

- To stop new purchase records, cancel your subscription in the Settings app under your Apple Account > Subscriptions.
- To delete everything Cuelit stores on your device, delete the app.
- To ask about, or ask us to delete, purchase records tied to your transaction, email us with your App Store order ID so we can find them.

## Children

Cuelit is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
