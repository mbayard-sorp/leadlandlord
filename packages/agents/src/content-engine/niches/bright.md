# Bright-Friendly Overlay (theme=bright)

This overlay tunes the content engine for D2C, family-friendly, in-home
service businesses where the relationship is built on trust, warmth, and
flexibility. The customer often has the service-provider in their home
when they're not there, around their pets and their kids and their
mother-in-law, on short notice. Niches in scope: house cleaning,
carpet and upholstery cleaning, window washing, dog walking, pet
grooming, mobile car detail, organizing and decluttering, mobile pet
wash, errand running and personal assistant services, party rentals
(bounce houses, tents, tables), event photography.

This buyer is choosing on chemistry, reviews, and reassurance. The
content should feel like a friendly local recommendation, not a
corporate pitch.

## Terminology

Use the language a homeowner would actually say to a friend at school
pickup. Plain words first; industry vocabulary only where it earns
its place.

- House cleaning: "deep clean vs maintenance clean", "move-in clean",
  "move-out clean", "post-construction clean", "weekly", "biweekly
  (every other week)", "monthly", "one-time", "checklist", "rotating
  tasks", "baseboards", "blinds", "inside the fridge", "inside the
  oven", "trash-out".
- Cleaning products: "low-toxicity products", "fragrance-free",
  "pet-safe", "kid-safe", "eco-friendly", "Green Seal certified",
  "EPA Safer Choice", "HEPA vacuum", "microfiber".
- Carpet and upholstery: "hot-water extraction", "steam cleaning",
  "encapsulation", "low-moisture clean", "stain protector",
  "Scotchgard", "deodorizer", "pet-stain treatment", "high-traffic
  lane cleaning", "drying time".
- Window cleaning: "interior + exterior", "screens in / screens out",
  "tracks and sills", "hard-water removal", "post-construction
  glass clean", "ladder-accessible vs lift-required", "skylights".
- Pet services: "in-home grooming", "mobile salon van", "deshedding",
  "nail trim", "anal gland expression" (use cautiously — only on
  service-detail pages), "ear cleaning", "tooth brushing", "puppy
  cut", "breed-standard cut", "matted coat surcharge", "leash
  walks", "off-leash play (where permitted)", "potty break visits",
  "overnight sitting".
- Mobile detail: "exterior wash", "interior detail", "clay bar",
  "compound and polish", "ceramic coating", "paint correction",
  "engine bay clean", "leather conditioning", "shampoo and
  extraction".
- Organizing: "sort, edit, contain, label", "donation haul-off",
  "pantry refresh", "closet edit", "playroom reset", "unpack
  service", "downsizing support".
- Party rentals: "drop-off and pickup", "setup and teardown",
  "delivery window", "weekend booking", "rain date policy",
  "permit-pull where required (bounce house, tent over a certain
  size)".
- Photography: "session length", "location scouting", "edited
  gallery", "delivery turnaround", "print release", "online proof
  gallery", "engagement / family / event packages".

## Seasonal Patterns

Bright-niche customers buy on calendar moments — make sure the content
reflects them.

- House cleaning: spring deep cleans (March–May); pre-Thanksgiving and
  pre-Christmas clean-ups; New Year reset clean; pre-listing real-estate
  cleans year-round; back-to-school refresh in August.
- Carpet cleaning: spring deep clean season; pre-holiday Q4; post-
  holiday January (after the parties).
- Window washing: spring (after the pollen) and fall (before storms);
  pre-listing for real estate; pre-event.
- Pet grooming: heavy pre-summer ("get the winter coat off");
  pre-holiday photos November–December; spring shedding.
- Dog walking: year-round, but back-to-school September spike (parents
  back at the office, dog needs the midday walk).
- Mobile car detail: spring "wake the car up", pre-trade-in, gift
  certificates Q4.
- Organizing: New Year fresh-start January, pre-school-start August,
  empty-nest summer, downsize / move season May–August.
- Party rentals: peak May–October (graduations, weddings, summer
  birthdays, fall fests); secondary spike December for holiday
  parties.
- Event photography: wedding season May–October, family portraits
  September–November (holiday cards), Mother's Day mini-sessions,
  spring milestone sessions.
- Pre-listing real-estate clean / detail / window jobs: year-round
  but follow the local market's busy season.

## Regulations to Reference Generically

This category has the lightest regulatory surface — emphasize
bonding, insurance, and trust signals over license numbers.

- Bonding and insurance — most important and most-asked. "Bonded and
  insured" should be visible early on every page.
- General liability insurance and (for employee-based companies)
  workers' compensation. Customers ask: "what if your cleaner gets
  hurt in my house?" Have an answer.
- IICRC certification for carpet cleaners (institute of inspection,
  cleaning, and restoration certification) — the recognized industry
  credential.
- State-specific pet groomer certifications and licensing — emerging
  in NY, NJ, CT, MA; voluntary in most states. National Dog Groomers
  Association of America (NDGAA) certified-master-groomer is the
  recognized credential.
- Pet-services bonding and pet-first-aid / CPR certifications
  (Pet Tech, Walks 'N' Wags) — strong trust signal.
- Food handlers permit for party rentals that include food service.
- Permit and inspection requirements for tents over certain
  square footage and bounce houses in certain jurisdictions.
- Background checks on staff who enter homes — strong reassurance,
  worth naming explicitly. E-Verify, motor-vehicle records for
  drivers.
- Photographer business license and (where applicable) commercial-use
  permits at parks and venues.
- Mobile detail / mobile pet wash — water reclamation rules in
  drought-restricted states (CA, AZ, NV); "we capture our water,
  no runoff to the storm drain".

## Common Customer Pain Points

- Trust in the home: "I work from home, who is this person who
  showed up?", "do I leave a key, a code, the garage opener?",
  "do they bring their own supplies?".
- Allergies and sensitivities: scented products, pet allergies in
  the home, kid-safe chemistry, fragrance-free options.
- Last-minute reschedules: customer side ("kid woke up sick, can we
  push to next week?") and provider side ("our van broke down,
  we'll make it up").
- Pet safety: "is your team okay with our anxious rescue?", "we
  have an escape-artist cat", "our dog jumps the fence".
- Kid safety: products on the floor, plug-in fragrances, vacuum
  cords, hot detail water on driveways where kids play.
- Consistent quality: "we love Maria but we got a different team
  last time and they missed the baseboards".
- Pricing transparency: hourly vs flat rate, what's included in
  "deep clean", what counts as an add-on (inside oven, inside
  fridge, garage).
- Communication: confirmation the night before, ETA window the
  day-of, who to text if running late.
- Photo / event timing: weather contingencies, golden-hour timing,
  rain dates, deposit refund policy.
- Pet grooming anxiety: long waits in the salon, separation, "can
  you do mobile so we don't have to take her in?".

## Common Objections to Address

1. **"How do I know your cleaners (or sitters, or detailers) are
   safe?"** — Background-checked, bonded and insured, named team
   leads, photo bios on the team page, every visit logged with
   in-and-out time.
2. **"What if I'm not happy?"** — 24-hour re-clean (or re-detail,
   or reshoot) guarantee. Specific commitment, not "we'll make
   it right".
3. **"Can I customize the package?"** — Yes. Add-ons listed clearly
   (inside oven, inside fridge, walk-in closets, garage); skip-the-
   guest-room option; product preferences (fragrance-free, eco-
   only, vinegar-only).
4. **"What products do you use?"** — Default to low-toxicity / pet-
   safe / kid-safe; provide product list on request; bring our
   own (or use yours if preferred).
5. **"Same person every time?"** — Best-effort yes; named primary
   with a backup. Notes follow the customer, not the cleaner —
   so the backup knows the dog's name and the alarm code.
6. **"What if I need to reschedule?"** — Clear policy, generous
   for first-time customers. 24-hour notice typical, no-penalty
   rescheduling within window.
7. **"What's your refund policy?"** — Specific. Deposit refundable
   up to X days. Service-day cancellation may incur a trip fee.
8. **"Do you bring supplies and equipment?"** — Yes by default;
   happy to use yours on request (eco-only households, scent-
   sensitive households).
9. **"Are you good with anxious pets?"** — Yes. Pet-first-aid
   certified, low-stress handling, can split sessions, can do
   mobile.
10. **"How fast can you book me?"** — Same-week typical, same-day
    for emergency (move-out, surprise guests). Online booking
    24/7.
11. **"What about my data and photos?"** — Photographer pages:
    clear print release, online gallery security, retention
    policy.

## Tone Notes

Bright-friendly voice is warm, accessible, and conversational without
being cute. Write like the neighbor who runs a small business well —
real names, real photos, real reviews, easy booking. Lead with the
customer's life (the holiday, the move, the new puppy, the parents
visiting) and slide the service in as a help, not a sell. Short
paragraphs, second person, plain words. Avoid corporate cleaning-
industry jargon ("hospitality-grade", "white-glove", "premium tier")
unless naming a specific package — those terms read cold here. Lean
into reassurance: bonded, insured, background-checked, satisfaction
guaranteed, easy to reach.
